# LEONIDA GOLD — Vice Cover Studio

Turn a photo and a caption into a GTA 6-style multi-panel cover: sunset and neon
grades, chrome 3D lettering, role tags, dialogue callouts, a seal and a billing
block. Export the cover as a PNG in three sizes: 1080&times;1080, 1080&times;1350
and 1920&times;1080.

## How to use

1. Open `app/index.html` in a desktop browser (or serve `app/` with any static
   server, e.g. `python3 -m http.server` inside `app/`).
2. Pick a sample photo or upload your own, choose a layout and a grade, edit
   the words, click a panel to crop it (zoom and pan).
3. Export PNG 1080&times;1080, 1080&times;1350 or 1920&times;1080 — or all three
   at once from the top toolbar.

## What is inside

- 8 panel layouts: Poster, Hero, Split Heist, Triptych, Quad, Spotlight,
  Strip, Duo Slant.
- 6 colour grades: Vice Sunset, Neon Nights, Palm Gold, Ocean Drive,
  After Dark, B&amp;W Noir.
- Chrome 3D title, role tags, dialogue callout, seal, billing block.
- Procedural scenery (skyline, palms, sun, ocean) drawn on canvas wherever a
  panel has no photo; the three sample photos are original illustrated Vice
  City scenes rendered in the browser.
- Fully offline: no network requests, no libraries. The Anton typeface ships
  as a file under `app/fonts/` under the SIL Open Font License.
